Patience

Klondike solitaire on a quiet linen table, for iPhone and iPad.

Draw one or three, standard or Vegas scoring, unlimited undo, honest hints, a daily deal, and winnable deals checked by a solver. No ads, no account, and nothing you do leaves your device.

How it is paid for

Patience starts with a free 7-day trial of the whole game. After seven days, one purchase unlocks it for good (shared through Family Sharing). There is no subscription. Statistics, settings and the rules stay open either way.

To move the game to a new device, open Settings in Patience and choose Restore purchases.
