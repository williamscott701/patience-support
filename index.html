Patience

Klondike solitaire on a quiet linen table, for iPhone and iPad.

A path of 120 levels through a day at the table, from First light to Midnight, each one proven winnable. A new daily challenge every day, with a streak. Free play with draw one or three, standard or Vegas scoring, unlimited undo and honest hints. No ads, no account and no tracking; anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off in Settings, help make the game better.

How it is paid for

No ads, ever. The first 10 days are free and include everything. After 10 days the whole game locks until you choose Full Deck: a monthly or yearly subscription, or one lifetime payment that keeps the game for good and is shared through Family Sharing. The app shows each price before you pay. Your levels, pips, streak and statistics are kept while the game is locked.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. To manage or cancel one, open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, or use Manage subscription in Patience's Settings. To move the game to a new device, choose Restore Purchases.
