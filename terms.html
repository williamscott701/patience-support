The 10-day Trial

The "10-day Trial" is a free in-app item. Taking it lets you play the whole game, every level, the daily challenge and free play, for 10 days (240 hours) from the moment you first take it. It costs nothing, never renews and turns into nothing paid. It can be taken once per Apple Account: reinstalling the app or restoring purchases does not start it again.

When the 10 days end, the whole game locks until you subscribe or buy it. Your levels, pips, streak, statistics and any game in progress stay on your device, and they open again as soon as you choose a plan.

Full Deck subscriptions

Full Deck Monthly and Full Deck Yearly are auto-renewable subscriptions that open the whole game for as long as they run. The price and the length of each are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically at the same price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your device's Settings, under your Apple Account, Subscriptions, or from Settings in Patience. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the game stays open until the end of the period you have paid for. The subscriptions have no free introductory period: the 10-day Trial already serves that purpose.

Full Deck Lifetime

Full Deck Lifetime is a one-time, non-consumable purchase at the price shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy. It opens the whole game permanently on every device signed in to the same Apple Account, and for members of your Family Sharing group. It is not a subscription and never renews. If you buy it while a subscription is running, cancel the subscription so that it does not renew.

Refunds and restoring

Purchases are made through Apple, and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a purchase is refunded, what it opened closes again. If you reinstall the app or move to a new device, choose Restore Purchases in Patience.
