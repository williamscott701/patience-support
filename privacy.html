Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Patience does not collect any data about you.

The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no account and no server. Your games in progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device, and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases (the free 7-day Trial and the full game) are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and tells the app only whether a purchase is active, so the app can unlock the game. We do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers those purchases.

Patience is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.
