Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Patience has no ads, no account and no tracking. It sends anonymous usage statistics, and you can turn them off.

On your device

Your games in progress, levels, pips, streaks, statistics and settings are stored only on your device, and are removed when you delete the app. There is no account and no server of ours.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see which levels are too hard, where players stop and whether the plans are clear, Patience sends anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. These are counts of events such as a level started or won (with its moves, time and pips), the daily challenge played, a hint or undo used, a setting changed, the plan screen seen, and a purchase made, with the country your device reports and basic device details such as the model and iOS version.

They are not linked to you. Patience sends no name, no email, no account, no user ID, no advertising identifier (the module that would read it is not included) and no identifier for this device's apps. Ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization are always denied, so nothing is used for advertising or shared with ad networks. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics storage is also denied, so Google receives only cookieless pings with no identifier at all. Patience does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

You can turn the statistics off at any time in Patience: Settings, About, Share anonymous usage. Google's own handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Purchases (the free 10-day Trial, the Full Deck subscriptions and the lifetime purchase) are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and tells the app only whether a purchase is active, so the app can open the game. We do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers those purchases.

Children

Patience is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
